Frequently Asked Questions

Waiver Policy

Can any dog come in for daycare?

Little dog daycare accepts dogs 18″ (paw-to-back) or under. Every dog does a Meet & Greet and a signed waiver before joining play.

What is the Meet & Greet?

An initial interview between the pup, the owners, and Dogs R Us Collingwood. It makes sure the pup is comfortable here, the owners are comfortable, and the pup is happy being handled by us.

Does the dog have to be a certain age?

Your pup must be at least 4 months old and have completed their second set of puppy vaccinations to join our daycare program.

My dog eats lunch. Can he have lunch at daycare?

Absolutely — and rest assured every dog eats separately, so no other dog will eat his food.

Are vaccinations required?

Yes. We require vaccination from a veterinarian for Rabies, DHPP/DA2PP (Distemper, Hepatitis/Adenovirus, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza), and Bordetella.
